Ancient Egyptian hieroglyphs were phonograms - they represented actual SOUNDS!

𓄿 = 'a' (glottal stop)
𓂋 = 'r' (alveolar tap)  
𓅱 = 'w' (labiovelar)
𓆑 = 'f' (labiodental)

Now you can HEAR them. Not just see them.
